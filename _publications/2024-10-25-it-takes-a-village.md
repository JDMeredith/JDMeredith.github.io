This paper evaluates a pilot project to improve maternal healthcare in rural Ghana.  The pilot involved both local community meetings that incorporated information about prenatal care and an enhanced care model that involved phone calls and a home visit by a community health worker.  We found that participants assigned to the enhanced care pilot were better prepared to manage complications during pregnancy and were more prepared for delivery day.
