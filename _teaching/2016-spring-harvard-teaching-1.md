This discussion-based seminar covered conventional topics in undergraduate labor economics, including minimum wages, immigration, and education.
The course focused on teaching the students to be effective consumers and producers of economics research.  As such, the curriculum was based around a series of research papers reflecting how our understanding of the topics have evolved over time.
